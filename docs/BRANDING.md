# Branding and Naming

## Selected working title

**Stellar Continuum**

Selected: 2026-09-07

Status: **official project working title; commercial naming clearance is not yet complete.**

The name was selected because:

- **Stellar** immediately communicates stars, space, and an interstellar/space-strategy genre.
- **Continuum** fits the core identity of a campaign that continues across centuries or millennia, changing governments, cultures, relationships, technologies, civilizations, galaxies, decline, recovery, and historical memory.
- The title does not assume the player is human, originates in Sol, uses a specific FTL technology, or pursues conquest as the only path.

Use the styling **Stellar Continuum** in project-facing documentation unless a later explicit naming decision supersedes it.

## Brand-positioning direction

The title should communicate a serious real-time civilization/grand-strategy game rather than a space shooter.

Possible supporting descriptors/taglines remain exploratory and are not locked:

- Real-Time Civilization Grand Strategy
- Build a Civilization. Shape its History.
- Civilizations Endure. History Continues.

The game title itself should remain uncluttered; descriptors/taglines can be used beneath it for store pages, trailers, websites, and key art.

## Preliminary naming-conflict check — 2026-09-07

This was a preliminary public-web check only. It is **not legal advice and not a trademark clearance opinion**.

### Game/store title search

No exact commercially listed video game titled **Stellar Continuum** surfaced in the searches run against Steam and other major game-index/store results on 2026-09-07.

This is encouraging but is not proof that no conflicting title, pending product, common-law mark, or unindexed use exists.

### Known non-identical uses

Known public uses of the wording include:

- **Stellar Continuum Gaming** — an established gaming/review/social brand that has used `stellarcontinuumgaming.com` and the social handle `@StellarCGaming`.
- **Stellar Continuum** — a 2026 music EP/song title by Vito Ruzzini.
- The phrase **stellar continuum** is also an ordinary astronomy/scientific term, so search-engine results naturally include scientific uses.

The existing **Stellar Continuum Gaming** name is the most relevant branding collision discovered so far because it operates in gaming/media. It is not the exact proposed game title, but it should be considered during trademark and marketing review.

### Trademark status

A preliminary public-web search did not surface an obvious exact U.S. trademark registration for **STELLAR CONTINUUM** covering video games/software.

That is **not sufficient clearance**. Before commercial launch, major branding spend, Steam announcement, crowdfunding, or filing our own mark, perform an official trademark search (including similar/confusing marks, not just exact wording) and obtain professional trademark advice if warranted.

### Domains and social handles

Exact domain/handle availability was not conclusively established by the available automated lookup tools.

Before public branding, check/register preferred properties directly through a registrar/platform. Candidate forms include:

- `stellarcontinuum.com`
- `stellarcontinuumgame.com`
- `playstellarcontinuum.com`
- `@StellarContinuumGame`
- `@PlayStellarContinuum`

Do not assume any of these are available until the registrar/platform confirms it at registration time.

## Naming guardrail

Until commercial clearance is complete:

- Treat **Stellar Continuum** as the canonical working title in source/docs/build UI.
- Do not represent the title as a registered trademark.
- Do not use ™/® based on this document alone.
- Do not rename the public GitHub repository solely for branding without an explicit decision; `afterburn25/game` remains the canonical repository path used by the handoff system.
- If a later clearance problem requires a different title, preserve this naming decision in history and explicitly supersede it rather than silently rewriting project records.
